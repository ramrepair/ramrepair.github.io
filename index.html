Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a repair cost?

Repair services at RAM Repair start at just $48! Final pricing depends on your device model, the repair required, and replacement-part costs.

We believe in honest, competitive pricing. Submit a free repair quote request with your device information, and we'll help you find an affordable solution.

How long will my repair take?

Most standard repairs are completed within 24–48 hours after local drop-off, depending on complexity, parts availability, and our current workload.

For nationwide mail-in repairs, turnaround times vary based on when your device arrives, the issue involved, and any required parts or diagnostics. We'll keep you informed if additional time is needed.

Do you offer local drop-off?

Absolutely! RAM Repair is proudly based in Chester, Virginia, serving Chester, Chesterfield, Richmond, and surrounding communities.

Local drop-off and pickup are available by appointment. Contact us or request a free quote to arrange a convenient time.

Can I mail in my device for repair?

You absolutely can! We offer nationwide mail-in repairs throughout the United States.

  1. Select Get Your Free Repair Quote.
  2. Enter your device details, describe the issue, and choose the mail-in service option.
  3. We'll review your request and provide pricing and shipping instructions.
  4. Once your device arrives, we'll inspect it, complete the approved repair, and arrange return shipping.

Do your repairs include a warranty?

Absolutely! We use reliable replacement parts and professional workmanship, and thoroughly test completed repairs before returning devices to customers.

Covered repairs include a 60-day limited repair warranty for eligible defects related to the completed repair or replacement parts. The warranty does not cover accidental or physical damage, liquid damage, misuse, or unrelated device issues occurring after service.

If your device needs additional service after the warranty period, please reach out. Depending on the circumstances, we may offer discounted repair pricing or another affordable solution.

What if my device has additional damage?

If we discover additional damage or unexpected issues, we'll explain our findings and discuss your options before proceeding.

Whenever possible, we'll work with you on an affordable solution, including potential discounts when combining repairs. No additional repairs or charges will be authorized without your approval.

Will I lose my photos, files, or saved data?

Many common hardware repairs do not normally require data removal, but some repairs, software procedures, or device conditions may affect stored information.

We recommend backing up important photos, contacts, files, and other data before service whenever possible. If your data is especially important, contact us before proceeding so we can discuss the risks of your specific repair.

What types of electronics do you repair?

We repair a wide range of electronics, including smartphones, tablets, gaming consoles, controllers, laptops, desktop computers, and other devices. We also perform select component-level and circuit-board repairs when practical and supported by parts availability.

If replacement parts are available and the repair is practical, there's a good chance we can help!

We do not currently service televisions, computer monitors, or printers. Not sure about your device? Send us a free quote request with its model and symptoms.
